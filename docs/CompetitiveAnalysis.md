## Executive Summary

The RAD and low-code platform market is projected to reach **$187 billion by 2030** (CAGR 31.1%). JAC-NEWGEN enters this space with a unique positioning: **full programming power meets 4GL ease**. Unlike proprietary low-code platforms that limit customization or traditional RAD tools that require extensive coding, JAC-NEWGEN bridges both worlds.

**Key Market Insights:**
- Enterprise adoption of low-code grew 23% in 2024
- Developer shortage driving demand for productivity tools (4M unfilled positions globally)
- 70% of organizations use at least one low-code platform
- Open-source alternatives growing 40% faster than proprietary solutions

**JAC-NEWGEN's Competitive Advantage:**
1. **Zero vendor lock-in** (open source, standard Java output)
2. **4GL productivity** with 3GL flexibility (no "ceiling" limitations)
3. **Zero build step** (instant JIT compilation)
4. **Enterprise-grade** from day one (logging, tracing, security)
5. **Cost advantage** (no per-user licensing, self-hosted)
